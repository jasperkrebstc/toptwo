T G drive · F H turn · B shoot
P4 middle right
I K drive · J L turn · N shoot

Seating runs left to right across the keyboard: P1 and P2 take the two ends, P3 and P4 the middle. Every player's shoot key sits inside their own cluster, so nobody reaches across anyone.

Double-tap turn-left or turn-right to sidestep. Double-tap and hold forward to sprint: faster, but a slower turn. Both burn stamina (the amber bar). Boxes stop players and bullets.

Each view keeps its own player centred and pointing up — the world turns underneath. Arrows on the edge point at opponents off screen.
